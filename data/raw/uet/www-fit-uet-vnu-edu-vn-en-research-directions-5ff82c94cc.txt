UET-FIT’s research interests cover almost all of aspects about information technology. UET-FIT has about 80 papers annually, which are published in different international journals and conferences. Moreover, many professors in UET-FIT are the heads of various important projects assigned by the Ministry of Science and Technology and Vietnam National University in Hanoi. UET-FIT’s goal is being the leading organization in researching and applying information technology in Vietnam.
